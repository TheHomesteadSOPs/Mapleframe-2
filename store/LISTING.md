# Gemhollow — store listing draft

**Title:** Gemhollow

**Short description (≈1 line):**
Match gems, smash skulls and cast elemental spells in this match-3 RPG battler.

**Description:**
Gemhollow is a fast, colorful match-3 puzzle RPG. Swap gems to line up three or more:
skulls strike your foe, while Fire, Air, Water and Earth gems charge your mana for powerful spells.
Chain cascades and four-gem matches to earn extra turns, level up your Wanderer, and battle through
a hollow of goblins, wolves, bandits, orcs, skeletons, trolls, wraiths and a dragon.

Features:
- Elemental match animations: flames, waves, gales, falling rocks and sword slashes
- Eight spells (Fireball, Heal, Frost, Smite, Wildfire, Cleanse, Stoneskin, Meteor) powered by gem mana, upgradeable in the shop
- Ten enemies with special moves, plus boss battles every five stages
- Enemy AI that plays the same board against you
- Saves your progress automatically; daily rewards, 13 achievements and an endless mode after the final boss
- Easy / Normal / Hard difficulty and 6 languages (English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Russian)
- 10 drum & bass tracks that change as you level up
- Plays on desktop and mobile, with fullscreen support

**Controls:** Click/tap a gem then an adjacent gem, or drag a gem toward its neighbor. Click a spell button when it glows. Keyboard: arrows + Space to swap, 1-8 for spells, Esc for the menu.

**Tags (suggested):** match 3, puzzle, rpg, strategy, fantasy, casual, battle, gems

**Languages:** English
